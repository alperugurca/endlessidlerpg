Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Endless Idle RPG is designed as an offline game.

Data Collection

The app does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Local Game Progress

Game progress is saved locally on the device using localStorage inside the app's WebView. Saved data may include player HP, attack, defense, level, XP, gold, enemy progress, purchased upgrade levels, and auto attack setting. This save data remains on the user's device and is not transmitted by the app.

Internet Access

The Android app is designed to work offline and does not request android.permission.INTERNET.

Analytics and Ads

The app does not include a runtime analytics SDK or advertising SDK.

Data Deletion

Users can delete local game progress by clearing the app's storage and cache from Android settings, uninstalling the app, or using any reset option available in the game.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information listed on the Google Play store page: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ae.endlessidlerpg
